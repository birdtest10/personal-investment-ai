# Source Quality and Data Hygiene

## Source hierarchy
1. Regulatory filings and official company investor-relations materials.
2. Official earnings releases, presentations, and transcripts.
3. Government, central-bank, and regulator datasets.
4. Reputable financial-data providers with transparent definitions.
5. Reputable financial journalism and research for context.
6. Social media/forums only as anecdotal sentiment, never as verified financial fact.

## Every time-sensitive analysis should state
- Date and time of research (include time zone)
- Price timestamp and source
- Financial reporting period
- Currency and units
- Whether estimates are consensus, company guidance, or analyst assumptions

## Cross-checks
- Check share count, stock splits, ADR ratios, fiscal year, and reporting currency.
- Reconcile GAAP vs non-GAAP.
- Check whether data is trailing twelve months, fiscal year, or calendar year.
- Confirm valuation multiples use consistent periods and definitions.
- Flag missing, conflicting, or stale information.

## Citation behavior
Cite important factual claims close to the claim. Prefer links to primary documents. Do not cite search snippets as if they were reviewed documents. If sources conflict, explain the discrepancy.
