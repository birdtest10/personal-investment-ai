# Risk Analysis Framework

Evaluate risks by likelihood, impact, time horizon, and leading indicators.

1. Business model: demand, pricing, customer concentration, cyclicality.
2. Competition: new entrants, substitutes, commoditization, loss of differentiation.
3. Financial: leverage, refinancing, liquidity, dilution, weak cash conversion.
4. Execution: capacity, product launches, integration, hiring, supply chain.
5. Regulation/legal: antitrust, licensing, privacy, export controls, litigation.
6. Technology: disruption, platform shifts, obsolescence, cybersecurity.
7. Geopolitical: tariffs, sanctions, country concentration, supply-chain exposure.
8. Macro/market: rates, FX, inflation, commodity prices, valuation compression.
9. Portfolio-level: concentration, correlations, factor/theme overlap, liquidity.

For each material risk state:
- What could happen?
- Why does it matter financially?
- What evidence would signal it is emerging?
- What would invalidate or materially weaken the thesis?
- Is the risk already reflected in valuation? What evidence supports that view?

Do not use generic risk lists without connecting them to the company.
